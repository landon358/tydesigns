TY Designs  ♡  Build plan

Instagram is the CMS. She posts, the site updates.

No admin panel, no second tool, no new habit — she already posts near-daily. The site reads her Instagram at request time through one small Netlify Function, so there are no deploys and no build credits burned. Stays on Netlify free.

Why no deploys matters: Netlify’s free plan is 300 credits/month and every production deploy is a flat 15 credits — about 20 publishes before the site pauses. Reading Instagram live sidesteps that entirely. Her photos are served from Instagram’s own CDN, so they don’t touch your bandwidth either.

The one reframe worth making

Her posts are not new products — they are new examples of the same ten products. A Hello Kitty tumbler post doesn’t mean “add a product,” it means “here’s another tumbler.” So the feed splits two ways:

Every post

Feeds the gallery

Automatic, zero effort, no tags needed. Newest twelve show on the home page and link back to the post.

Tagged #tyshop

Becomes a shop card

Opt-in, so a random selfie or a “happy Friday” post never lands in the catalog.

Her caption convention

Instagram gives us one blob of caption text, not fields. So the function reads it by position and by tag — and she only has to remember this when she wants a post in the shop.

Custom Photo Tumbler, 20 oz        ← line 1 becomes the product name
Add your favorite picture and name. ← line 2 becomes the one-line note
Prices starting at $20              ← any $ amount becomes the price

#tyshop #tumbler                    ← #tyshop = put it in the shop
                                       #tumbler = which category
